APPLE INC. - ANNUAL REPORT & 10-K HIGHLIGHTS

Fiscal Year Financial Summary:
- Total Net Sales: $383.285 Billion (Up 6.5% YoY)
- Products Revenue: $298.085 Billion
- Services Revenue: $85.200 Billion (+14.2% YoY, Gross Margin of 71.3%)
- Gross Margin: $169.148 Billion (44.1% of sales)
- Operating Income: $114.301 Billion (Operating Margin: 30.1%)
- Net Income: $96.995 Billion (Diluted EPS: $6.13)
- Cash Flow from Operations: $110.543 Billion
- Free Cash Flow: $99.584 Billion

Balance Sheet & Capital Returns:
- Cash, Cash Equivalents & Marketable Securities: $162.1 Billion
- Total Commercial Paper & Term Debt: $111.088 Billion
- Total Stockholders' Equity: $62.146 Billion (Debt-to-Equity: 1.45x)
- Share Repurchases: $77.550 Billion
- Dividends Paid: $15.025 Billion (Annual Dividend Yield: 0.58%, Payout Ratio: 15.2%)

Competitive Position & Economic Moat:
- Active installed base exceeding 2.2 Billion devices worldwide.
- High switching costs, unified hardware-software ecosystem (iOS, macOS, watchOS), and custom silicon (M-series / A-series).
- Continuous services expansion (iCloud, Apple Pay, App Store, Apple TV+, Apple Care).

Key Risk Factors:
- Antitrust and regulatory challenges surrounding App Store commissions and default search agreements.
- Geopolitical dependencies on supply chains in East Asia.
- Extended upgrade cycles for premium consumer electronics.
