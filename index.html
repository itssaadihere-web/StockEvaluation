n8n Connection & Multi-Device Setup

For live Vercel usage on other devices, use an HTTPS tunnel URL (ngrok/Cloudflare).

How to connect from any device to your local n8n:
  1. Run a free tunnel on your PC: n8n start --tunnel or ngrok http 5678
  2. Copy the HTTPS tunnel URL provided in your terminal.
  3. Append /webhook/compare-reports and paste it here on any device.
  4. Whenever you upload reports on Vercel from your phone/tablet, it triggers your local n8n automations directly!
